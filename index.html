01 · About Me

Data analyst, builder, and a fast learner by necessity.

I'm a B.Tech Information Technology graduate (CGPA 8.52) from Chennai, and I got into data analysis the way most good analysts do, by getting curious about numbers that didn't add up. I've spent the last stretch building three full case studies from scratch: cleaning genuinely messy multi-table data, running real statistical tests to back up every claim, and shipping finished Power BI and Tableau dashboards, not just charts.

What I actually enjoy is the moment a "surprising" finding turns out to be real once you check it properly, like discovering the airline everyone blames isn't actually the worst performer, or that a "loyalty" segment is really just big first-order spenders. I'd rather ship an honest, slightly uncomfortable insight than a polished one that doesn't hold up under a follow-up question.
